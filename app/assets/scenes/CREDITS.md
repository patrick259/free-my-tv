# Scenes

`night.jpg`, `night-glass.jpg` and `night-fg.webp` come from [couch-tv](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv)
(`tv-home/web/backgrounds/scene-night*`). Its credits say these scenes were made for couch-tv with an AI image model
from text only, are not real places, and may be used however you like; the glass copy and the cut-out (the scenery
with the sky transparent, drawn over the clock) are made from the same scene under the same terms.
