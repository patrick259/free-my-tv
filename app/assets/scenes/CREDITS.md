# Scenes

The scenes (`night`, `golden`, `water`, `fog`, `dawn`: each a `.jpg`, a `-glass.jpg`, and for all but `water` a
`-fg.webp`) come from [couch-tv](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv) (`tv-home/web/backgrounds/scene-*`). Its credits
say these scenes were made for couch-tv with an AI image model from text only, are not real places, and may be used
however you like; the glass copies and the cut-outs (the scenery with the sky transparent, drawn over the clock) are
made from the same scenes under the same terms. The clock's place in each follows couch-tv's own (app.css).
